BEFORE DEPLOYMENT
----------------------

- [ ] Set the three Resend variables in your host's environment settings, along with the two Sanity ones.

- [ ] Verify a domain in Resend and change CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL, so messages can be sent to trevor

- [ ] Add your live site URL to Sanity's CORS origins if you hit a CORS error on the deployed site. 
      Your sanityFetch runs on the server so you may not need it.
